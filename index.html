Method and decision guardrails

The calculator uses a uniform Beta(1,1) prior and 100,000 Monte Carlo draws from each Beta posterior. “Probability best” is the share of simulations in which a variant has the highest sampled conversion rate. Reaching the probability threshold is a decision signal, not a guarantee. Also apply a pre-agreed minimum runtime, sample requirement, primary metric and practical uplift threshold before ending a test.
